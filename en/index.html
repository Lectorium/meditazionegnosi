We do not belong to this world. Gnosis is the awakening of Spirit and the path back to the Homeland of Light.

Why this website?

This world is not our homeland. Matter is the realm of corruption, domination, and death: a prison we refuse to celebrate or make spiritually acceptable. In the dualist Gnostic vision at the heart of this website, the good Father is foreign to material creation, and Spirit carries within it the call of the Light. Gnosis is the awakening through which we recognize our condition and seek liberation.
