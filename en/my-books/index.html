MEDITAZIONE E GNOSI

My books

For those wishing to explore these themes further

Three works written at different stages of my journey, before and after my encounter with Gnosis. I do not wish to turn every earlier experience retrospectively into doctrine: the thread I now recognize is the question of our origin, Spirit’s estrangement from this world, and the possibility of liberation from matter.

01

Sulle Ali di Dio

Diary from my hermitage, towards Gnosis

Silence, peace, knowledge. After an intense and often disillusioning search that began in childhood, I left my job at the age of twenty-seven to live in a hermitage. That decision sparked a series of profound and varied spiritual experiences.

02

Stranieri di passaggio

A journey towards liberation

An inner journey begun in 2007, before I encountered Gnosis, but already shaped by the intuition that this world is not our true home. A symbolic novel about the human journey, the bridge, and the search for freedom. The book is no longer distributed through bookshops. If you would like to request a copy, please contact me through the contact form.

03

Esegesi del Vangelo di Tommaso secondo uno gnostico

A reading to live by

I am not a historian, nor a lover of human culture. But I love freedom, and this Gospel is full of it. I try to share that freedom not as a scholar, but as a Gnostic. This is not a book to leave on a shelf: it is meant to be lived. I have written what is essential; the rest is up to you.
