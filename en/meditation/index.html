MEDITAZIONE E GNOSI

Meditation

Silence as a starting point

Meditation is not an escape from the world, nor a technique for convincing ourselves that everything is fine. It is an opportunity to observe more clearly what happens within us: thoughts, fears, desires, and automatic reactions.

Observing without identifying

We can begin with a few minutes of silence, sitting comfortably. When a thought arises, there is no need to fight it: simply recognize it and return to attention. Over time it becomes easier to distinguish what passes through us from what we are.

Meditation and Gnosis

On our path, silence is not a way to make peace with this world. It helps expose the conditioning that binds us to matter. Meditation accompanies Gnosis: it helps us distinguish the noise of the mind from the call of Spirit, whose homeland is not here.
