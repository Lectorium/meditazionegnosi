History of Gnosis

Gnosis is not a universal spirituality in which all doctrines become interchangeable. This history follows traditions that questioned the dominion of the visible world, the captivity of Spirit, and the possibility of liberation. Our standpoint is radical dualism: the good Father is not the maker of this material order. Historical accuracy nevertheless requires distinguishing schools that did not all teach two eternal principles.

Spiritual roots

Zoroaster, Orphism, Pythagoras, Plato, ancient mysteries, India, and Alexandria.

Teachers and schools

Simon Magus, Basilides, Valentinus, the Sethians, and other movements of the early centuries.

From Mani to the Cathars

Manichaeism, the Bogomils, the Cathars, and the legacy of Gnosis.
