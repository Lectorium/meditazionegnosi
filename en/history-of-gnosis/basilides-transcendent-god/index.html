MEDITAZIONE E GNOSI

Basilides and the Transcendent God

A teacher in Alexandria

Basilides taught in Alexandria in the second century. His doctrine is known chiefly through writers who summarized and criticized it, not always consistently. Reconstructing a single system in every detail is therefore difficult, but his teaching centres on the transcendence of the divine principle and the complexity of cosmic orders.

Beyond ordinary images of God

Ancient reconstructions describe a principle beyond familiar categories. The divine cannot be reduced to an enlarged ruler resembling human beings. This distance raises questions about the relationship between the supreme principle, lesser powers, and the world in which we live.

Saving knowledge

The spiritual significance of Basilides lies in his invitation not to confuse the visible order with the absolute. From this perspective, religious knowledge is not merely a doctrine to repeat: it is a path that changes how we understand human origins and destiny.
