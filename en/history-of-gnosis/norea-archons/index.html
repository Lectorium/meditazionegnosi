MEDITAZIONE E GNOSI

Norea and the Archons

Rulers of the world

The Hypostasis of the Archons reworks and transforms biblical accounts of origins. The Archons are powers that dominate the world and human beings, but they are not the supreme divine principle. Their power is bound up with ignorance and an attempt to keep humanity in subordination.

The figure of Norea

Norea resists the claims of the lower powers and becomes a figure of spiritual resistance. Her role is not simply rebellion for its own sake: she refuses to treat an authority lacking truth as absolute.

The meaning of resistance

The story raises an essential question: how can we distinguish humanity’s true origin from the forces that seek to define and possess it? Spiritual autonomy emerges through the language of myth.
