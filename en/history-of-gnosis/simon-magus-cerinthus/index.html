MEDITAZIONE E GNOSI

Simon Magus and Cerinthus

A controversial name

Simon Magus appears in the Acts of the Apostles and in writings by Christian authors who opposed him. His figure must therefore be approached carefully: the teacher’s own voice has not reached us directly. According to doctrines attributed to him, the Great Power is the supreme principle, while Thought, Ennoia, becomes involved in a drama of descent and forgetfulness.

Helen and forgetfulness

The story of Helen, identified with Ennoia, portrays a spiritual reality fallen into the world. Forgetfulness becomes central: human beings no longer recognize their origins. Liberation is described as recovering knowledge, not simply obeying an external authority.

Cerinthus and the creator

Cerinthus, known chiefly through hostile accounts, is associated with a distinction between the supreme God and the powers responsible for the world. His teaching raises a question that would recur across many schools: is the creator of the visible world truly the ultimate principle?
