MEDITAZIONE E GNOSI

Justin the Gnostic and Bardaisan

Justin the Gnostic

In the narrative attributed to Justin the Gnostic, the relationship between the creator, cosmic powers, and a higher good is presented through a distinctive myth. His importance lies not in founding a dominant school, but in offering another answer to the problem of a world in which power does not necessarily coincide with knowledge of the good.

Bardaisan of Edessa

Bardaisan lived between the second and third centuries in the Syriac environment of Edessa. His thought engaged with astrology and the problem of fate, defending a real place for human freedom. It would be misleading to reduce him automatically to a uniform Gnostic doctrine, but his teaching belongs to the wider debate about the human condition and the forces that seem to govern it.

Freedom and responsibility

Bringing these two authors together shows that salvation is not only a question of the structure of heaven. It also concerns the ability to choose, to understand, and not to accept passively what is presented as inevitable.
