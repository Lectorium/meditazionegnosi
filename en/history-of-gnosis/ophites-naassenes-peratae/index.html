MEDITAZIONE E GNOSI

Ophites, Naassenes, and Peratae

The serpent reinterpreted

Some movements known as Ophites and Naassenes interpreted the serpent differently from its traditional condemnation. In the Eden story, the search for knowledge could become a sign of awakening against the claims of a lower authority. These communities did not all share the same doctrine, and their names often come from outside classifications.

Self-knowledge

In materials associated with the Naassenes, mythical language intertwines with different religious and philosophical images. The search for humanity’s origin takes a central place. The point is not to worship an animal or invert a symbol for provocation, but to ask what knowledge truly leads to freedom.

The Peratae and passage beyond

The Peratae developed distinctive images of passing beyond the world and its powers. Their name evokes the theme of crossing over: not remaining imprisoned in the present condition. These schools illustrate the variety of languages in which Gnosis has expressed the path of liberation.
