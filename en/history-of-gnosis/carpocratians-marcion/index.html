MEDITAZIONE E GNOSI

The Carpocratians and Marcion

The Carpocratians

Accounts of the Carpocratians, mostly polemical, describe a movement that challenged worldly hierarchies and gave knowledge a decisive role. Because the sources are hostile, not every accusation should be taken as a faithful portrait of their lives. Their place among radical interpretations of spiritual freedom remains significant.

Marcion’s alien God

Marcion proposed a sharp distinction between the good God revealed by Jesus Christ and the lawgiving God of the Hebrew scriptures. His position was not simply identical to that of all Gnostic schools, but it forcefully raised the question of the relationship between justice, law, and divine goodness.

What was at stake

In different ways, these movements asked whether worldly and religious authority could automatically be equated with truth. Their conflict with emerging Christianity therefore concerned the very meaning of liberation.
