MEDITAZIONE E GNOSI

The Sethians and the Apocryphon of John

Descendants of light

In texts now described as Sethian, Seth plays a symbolic role associated with a spiritual lineage. The biblical story is reinterpreted through a cosmology in which the supreme God transcends the material world. Salvation is connected with recognizing one’s origin and being freed from ignorance.

Yaldabaoth and the creator

In the Apocryphon of John, Yaldabaoth appears as a lower power who proclaims himself the only deity. His error lies in ignorance of the higher reality. The narrative overturns an ordinary assumption: possessing the power to rule the world does not mean knowing the ultimate principle.

The text’s message

The Apocryphon of John describes a complex universe, but its spiritual core is clear. Human beings are more than the powers of this world claim them to be. Revelation does not merely add information: it reveals the possibility of awakening.
