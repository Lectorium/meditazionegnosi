MEDITAZIONE E GNOSI

The path of knowledge

Gnostic catechesis · Dualist tradition

Gnosis is not merely the study of ancient texts or adherence to a doctrine. It is a path of inner knowledge through which human beings may recognize their spiritual origin and understand their condition.

This catechesis continues the path begun in 2009 with Chiesa Catara, developing its teachings from a Gnostic perspective of absolute dualism.

At the heart of our vision is the distinction between the good Father, the principle of Light and Spirit, and the principle of Evil, to which material creation belongs.

We do not consider this world our true homeland. The spiritual path does not consist in perfecting the material prison, but in recognizing its nature and rediscovering the way to liberation.

The teachings that follow are arranged in progressive chapters. We do not ask for blind adherence, but invite reflection, meditation, and inwardly lived knowledge.
