MEDITAZIONE E GNOSI

Gnostic library

Texts, voices and images of the inner search

Not a collection of consolations, but witnesses to a search for liberation.

This library does not indiscriminately celebrate religions. It gathers ancient Gospels, Gnostic writings, and dualist testimonies in the light of a radical question: who holds us in this world, and where does the Spirit that feels alien here come from? These texts are not cultural ornaments, but traces of a search for liberation.
