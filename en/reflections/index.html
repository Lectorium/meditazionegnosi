MEDITAZIONE E GNOSI

Reflections

Looking at the world without justifying it

Italiano · Français

Alongside the Catechesis and Gnostic sources, I want to gather reflections arising from everyday life, meditation, books, and cinema. Not to reconcile ourselves with the world, but to expose its illusions, its violence, and the bonds that hold us in matter.

Our standpoint is radical dualism: the good Father did not make this world, and Spirit does not belong here. Gnosis is not meant to decorate the prison but to recognize it. Even a work that is not Gnostic can sometimes help us glimpse this rupture.

Articles and meditations

I will gradually publish writings on the human condition, radical dualism, Christ’s message, nonviolence, and Gnostic resonances in literature and cinema.
